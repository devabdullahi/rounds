Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Rounds is a study app for medical students: you interview fictional AI patients, order tests, make a diagnosis and get feedback. This policy explains what information Rounds collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. Rounds is provided by Abdullahi Khalafalla ("we", "us"). Questions: tryroundsapp@gmail.com.

The short version: we collect what's needed to run your account and your practice cases. We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

Every patient is fictional. Don't enter real patient information.

Rounds is for education only. All patients in Rounds are made up. Never type, say or scan information about real patients, or your own private health information, into Rounds. Rounds is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.

We do not collect your payment card details. We don't use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps and websites, and we don't collect your location or contacts. The only usage measurement is on the subscription screen, described above.

How your information is processed

  • Patient conversations and grading use AI. When you talk to a patient, finish a case, or make flashcards, the relevant text (for example your question, your diagnosis, or your notes) is sent to our AI provider, DeepSeek, to write the patient's reply, the feedback or the cards. DeepSeek processes and may store this text on servers in the People's Republic of China, under its own privacy policy. We send only the text needed for that request, never your email address or name.
  • Voice mode uses Apple's speech recognition, which may send your audio to Apple to turn it into text (see Apple's privacy policy). The patient's spoken replies are made on your device.
  • Scanned notes are read into text on your device. Only that text is sent to make cards. The photo itself stays on your device.
  • Medical reference lookups (diagnosis search, medicine names and "learn more" reading) send the search words, not who you are, to the U.S. National Library of Medicine's public services.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run Rounds, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase (database, accounts and file storage, hosted in the United States)
  • DeepSeek (AI for patient replies, grading and flashcards, described above)
  • Amazon Web Services (sending sign-in codes and account emails)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, speech recognition) and Google (Sign in with Google, if you choose it)
  • Superwall (shows the subscription screen and measures how it's used, as described above; it never receives your payment details)
  • Cloudflare (hosts this website)

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of our users or Rounds.

Payments

You subscribe in the app, and Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only your plan, its status and its dates.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, practice history, scores, flashcards and badges from our systems. Copies in backups are removed as those backups expire. Apple keeps its own records of purchases as the law requires. Text already sent to DeepSeek is kept under DeepSeek's policy.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and data.
  • See or correct your information: your profile and progress are in the app. For a copy of your data, or to correct something you can't change yourself, email us.
  • Notifications are optional. Turn them off in Settings in the app or on your iPhone.
  • Voice mode, the microphone and the camera are optional. Rounds asks before using them, and you can turn them off in your iPhone's Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have rights to know, access, correct or delete your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using them. We don't sell or share personal information as those laws define it. To make a request, email tryroundsapp@gmail.com. We'll answer within 30 days.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Your data is protected by access rules so that only you can see your own account, and service keys are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique email account and keep your device locked.

Age

Rounds is for medical students and isn't intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: tryroundsapp@gmail.com.
