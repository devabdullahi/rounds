The patient

Knows only what a real patient would.

No multiple choice and no hints. The patient answers what you ask, in plain words, and never gives the diagnosis away. You do the thinking.

  • Talk, don't click

    Type your questions or ask out loud, the way you would at the bedside.

  • Examine and order tests

    Pick your maneuvers and labs. Results come back in real units: CBC, BMP, mg/dL.

  • Commit, then get graded

    Name the diagnosis and a plan. Your attending scores it and shows what you missed.

  2. ExamineChoose your maneuvers
