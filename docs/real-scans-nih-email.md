**Subject:** Use of the NIH ChestX-ray14 images in a medical education app

Hello,

I build Palpo, an iOS app where US medical students practice clinical reasoning on fictional patient
cases. We would like to show a small, curated set of images from the NIH ChestX-ray14 dataset (about
100–300) when a student orders a chest X-ray in a practice case.

How we would use them:

- Images are shown inside the app to signed-in students, including paying subscribers.
- They are labeled as real de-identified teaching images, never as the fictional patient's own scan.
- We re-encode them (resize, strip metadata) and do not redistribute the dataset itself.
- We credit the NIH Clinical Center as data provider, link to the NIH download site, and cite
  Wang et al., CVPR 2017, in each caption's details and in the app's acknowledgements.
- We will not state or imply that NIH endorses the app.

The dataset documentation says there are no restrictions on use of the images, with attribution. Could
you confirm that this use in a commercial education app is consistent with those terms, or tell us of
any condition we should follow?
